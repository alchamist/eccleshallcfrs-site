**A big thank you to Recycled Furniture UK for supporting Eccleshall Community First Responders.**

As work continues to get our new premises ready, we're very grateful to Steve at Recycled Furniture UK for generously donating two office tables for our new training room.

The tables are already in place and will provide us with a dedicated workspace for responder training, meetings and the ongoing development of our volunteers.
Having our own premises is an exciting step forward for Eccleshall Community First Responders, but creating a practical and well-equipped base takes much more than simply having somewhere to park our response vehicle. Donations of equipment, furniture and services from local businesses help us make the most of the funds entrusted to us and allow more of our resources to remain focused on supporting our community.

Recycled Furniture UK supplies quality recycled office furniture throughout Staffordshire, Shropshire and Cheshire, helping businesses and organisations give furniture a new lease of life while reducing unnecessary waste.

**Thank you to Steve and Recycled Furniture UK for your generous support — the tables will be put to very good use!**
