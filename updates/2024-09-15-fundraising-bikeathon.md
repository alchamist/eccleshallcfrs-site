Steve Dono from Eccleshall is fundraising for Eccleshall First Responders by taking on the Henley-On-Thames aqua bike event — a gruelling combination of a 2k swim and a bikeathon.

Every penny raised goes directly towards keeping the scheme running, funding our vehicle, equipment, and uniforms.

If you'd like to sponsor Steve and support the scheme, please visit our JustGiving page — and thank you for your support!
