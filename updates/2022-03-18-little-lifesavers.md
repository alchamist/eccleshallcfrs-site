Today, Eccleshall Community First Responders and Stone Community First Responders joined forces to present a 'people who help us' session to a group of Reception and Nursery Class children at a local school.

The session introduced the children to the emergency services and community volunteers who help keep them safe, and gave them a simple introduction to what to do in an emergency — including the importance of calling 999.

It's never too early to start learning about how to help in an emergency. We're proud to play a part in the community beyond just responding to calls.
