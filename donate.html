Support Eccleshall CFRs

Every donation helps us keep responding — and keep saving lives.

Why Your Donation Matters

We receive no funding from central sources. Our volunteers give their time freely — but running the scheme costs money. Your donation directly funds the things that keep Eccleshall CFRs operational.

What we spend money on

  • 🚗 Response vehicle — purchase, insurance, fuel, and maintenance
  • 🩺 Response equipment — defibrillator, patient monitor, and response bag: significant one-off purchases that last many years
  • 👕 Uniforms — each full uniform set (shirts, trousers, boots, coat, and hi-vis) costs around £300 per responder
  • 📋 Scheme costs — insurance, admin, and day-to-day running costs add up to around £1,000 a month to keep the scheme operational
